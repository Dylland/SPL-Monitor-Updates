SPL Monitor 0.9.0-beta.4-dev112 (closed beta, Apple silicon Macs)

Install: open the DMG and drag SPL Monitor to Applications. The beta isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first launch is blocked: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to SPL Monitor. The Install Guide has pictures.

New in this build
- Calibrate first: until you do, readings show plain dBFS, marked "Uncalibrated · dBFS".
- A calibrator on the mic pauses readings and crew dose on its own. Turn off Calibrator pause (Visual Settings) before running sine sweeps.
- Saving a calibration starts the readings over and leaves a note with the time and amount in the report.
- Reset the averages: tap an LAeq number twice, or press Z twice.
- RTA range in Visual Settings, including dBFS down to -140.
- More accurate A and C weighting above 8 kHz.

Feedback: use the Feedback button in the app. It goes straight to the developer.
