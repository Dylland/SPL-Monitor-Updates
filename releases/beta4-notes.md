SPL Monitor 0.9.0 Beta 4, for the closed beta. Mac, Apple Silicon.

**Install.** Open the DMG and drag SPL Monitor onto Applications. macOS warns once because the app isn't notarized yet: right-click the app, Open, then Open again. "Read Me First" in the DMG walks through it.

**What's in it.** A live SPL meter that opens to your numbers once your rig is saved. LAeq 15 (and short and long windows), live LCpeak with the 15-minute max, an analyzer with target curves as a tonal reference, NIOSH and OSHA crew dose, Record with venue and crew PDF reports, Stream Deck control through Bitfocus Companion, and a phone view. A short tour is offered after setup; take it once.

**Feedback.** Setup > Feedback inside the app. It sends your note, your setup details and today's level logs (numbers only, never audio). After a crash the app asks once whether to send the report; please say yes.

**Known limits.** Not notarized yet. Apple Silicon only in this build. Auto-Find can interrupt audio in other apps, so it stays off unless you tick it. Not a certified sound level meter: accuracy depends on your mic and calibration.

Beta 4 is free, and every tester keeps full access.
